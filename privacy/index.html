  • We collect the information you give us (forms, emails, agreements, requests) and what we need to take payments and deliver your project.

Who we are

Valeur Studio ("we", "us") designs and builds websites, custom packaging and launch systems for small businesses, from [Tampa Bay, Florida; confirm]. [Legal entity name, mailing address]. Questions about this policy or your information: [privacy contact email, for example kazan@valeurstudio.com].

Where this policy applies

  • Our website, valeurstudio.com, including the Free Website Audit.
  • Client pages: the private links we send you for proposals, agreements, payments, website review, support requests, billing and Additional Work approvals.
  • Our emails and the information we keep to run the business and work with you.

It does not cover websites we build for clients, which have their own policies, or other sites we link to, such as Stripe and Instagram.

Information we collect, and why

Our website and its forms.

  • Start a Project form: your name, email address, business name, the service you are interested in, and the description you write. Your message is emailed to us through Resend (an email delivery service) and added to our client records.
  • Free Website Audit (valeurstudio.com/audit): your website address, business name and type, what you sell, who your customers are, your main goal, your name, email address, phone number (optional) and any notes. To produce the audit, our server (1) looks at your website: your home page, whether some links on it work, and your robots.txt and sitemap files; (2) sends your website address to Google's PageSpeed Insights service to measure speed; and (3) sends your business name, business type, what you sell, who your customers are, your goal and up to the first 6,000 characters of text from your page to Anthropic's AI service (Claude) to help score parts of the audit. Your name, email address and phone number are not sent to Google or Anthropic. The result is shown to you, emailed to us together with everything you entered (through Resend), and saved in our client records. It is also kept in your browser's session storage for the visit so the results page can display it; that stays on your device.
  • The website does not use cookies. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs of requests (see "Information from our systems and providers").

Information you give us when we work together.

  • The business and contact details we need to prepare proposals and agreements, scope notes, and files or links you share (see "Shared folders").
  • When you review, approve or sign: your typed name and email, the date and time, the statements you agreed to, and, if you draw one, your signature image.
  • When you send a revision or support request: your name, email, and the text of the request.
  • When you tell us you sent a Zelle payment: the amount and the time you say you sent it.

Records we keep as evidence that you agreed. When you sign an agreement, approve a proposal, a revision round or Additional Work, or authorize new billing terms, we also record your IP address and your browser's user agent text, with the time. We keep these so that we can show who agreed to what and when.

Payment information. Cards, and for eligible one-time payments Klarna, are handled by Stripe (and Klarna, through Stripe). We never see or store your full card number. We store the amount, the status, Stripe's reference numbers for the payment and customer, and, for monthly billing, the card brand, last four digits and expiry month and year. If you pay by Zelle, we confirm the payment ourselves; we do not receive your bank login.

Shared folders. For asset sharing we may create a Google Drive folder and send you its link. Anyone who has that link can view and add files. The folder is not publicly searchable, but you should treat the link like a password.

Emails. We send email from our own Google Workspace account. We keep a copy of the emails and drafts we send about your project. Our system only sends email; it does not read our inbox. Replies arrive in our email account. We do not use tracking pixels or open-tracking in our emails.

Booking. If we invite you to book a walkthrough, the booking page is a Google Calendar appointment page, so Google receives what you enter there.

Activity on the links we send you ("link activity"). When you open a client page, and it stays on your screen for about five seconds, we record that the link was opened: which link or page, the date and time, and the project it belongs to. We group openings into "sessions": everything opened on a project until 30 minutes pass without activity counts as one session, so our team is told once, not on every refresh. We keep every opening in the project's history.

  • This is tied to you, because the link was made for you. It is not anonymous.
  • It is not proof of who opened the page: anyone who has the link could have.
  • We do not store your IP address, device details or browser details for link activity. Our servers read your browser's "user agent" text once to filter out automated programs such as search crawlers and link scanners, and we do not keep it.
  • We do not use cookies, or save anything on your device, for link activity.
  • When you press the button to open a design preview, we record that click the same way.

Notifications to our own team. When something happens on a client page (an approval, a payment, a request, or a link being opened), our team may receive a notification on their own phone or computer. These can include your business name and what happened. They are delivered through the push services of Apple, Google or Mozilla.

Information from our systems and providers. Our hosting and database providers keep standard server logs, which can include IP addresses, request times and browser user agents. We use them for security and troubleshooting.

Referrals. If someone refers you to us, or you refer someone, we keep the referrer's name, email and phone for the referral record and any payout.

Reaching out to businesses

We also contact some businesses we have not worked with before. Here is how that works.

  • Where we find businesses. We use listings on Google Maps (collected through a data-collection service), the business's own public website, and, sometimes, lists we upload ourselves. We record the business name, address, phone, website, listing link, rating and review count, social media links, and, where a business publishes them, contact details such as an email address and the names or roles of owners or staff, including links to public professional profiles. To collect from a business's own website, our system reads its home page and up to four other pages.
  • How we contact them. We write and send these emails one at a time from our own email account. We are not a bulk email service.
  • If you do not want to hear from us. Email [privacy contact email] and tell us, and we will not contact you again. [OWNER TO CONFIRM that this is how requests are handled. There is no automated do-not-contact list, and the emails carry no unsubscribe line. Counsel to review; see the reviewer notes.]
  • Your information. You can ask what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, as described under "Your choices and rights".

Cookies and similar technologies

  • Client pages do not set cookies and do not use your browser's local or session storage for tracking.
  • One exception on client pages, which you control: the website review form saves a draft of your feedback in your browser (local storage) so that you do not lose it if the page reloads. It stays on your device and is cleared when you submit.
  • Our website does not set cookies. Its fonts (Google Fonts) and one photograph on the home page (Unsplash) load from those companies' servers, so they receive your IP address and browser details when you visit, as any website's third-party content provider does. The Free Website Audit keeps your result in session storage for the visit.
  • No analytics or advertising trackers run on our website. [Google Tag Manager and Meta Pixel code exists in the site's source but is switched off. It must not be switched on without updating this policy and deciding on consent. Re-verify before publishing.]
  • Stripe sets its own cookies when you are on a Stripe page to pay. Those are Stripe's, under its policy.
  • Our team uses sign-in cookies in our internal system. Clients do not sign in.

  • To take payments, send receipts and manage monthly billing.
  • To communicate with you by email about your project and, where it applies, to follow up on an enquiry or proposal.
  • To keep a record of what was agreed, and to protect against fraud and misuse (including filtering automated traffic).
  • To let our team know when a client has opened a link or taken an action, so we can respond promptly.
  • To find and contact businesses that may want our services, as described above.
  • To keep our business records and meet legal, tax and accounting duties.
