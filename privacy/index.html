Legal

Privacy Policy

How Valeur Studio collects, uses and protects personal information on this website, in our client pages and in our emails.

Effective date: October 20, 2026. Last updated: October 20, 2026.

The short version

  • We collect the information you give us and what we need to run your project and take payments.
  • When you open a private link we send you, we note that it was opened. This is not anonymous.
  • Our website and client pages do not use cookies, and we do not use analytics or advertising trackers.
  • We never see your full card number.
  • We use third-party technology and service providers to run our business and to produce our free website audit. This policy says what is shared.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • Questions or requests: kazan@valeurstudio.com.

Who we are

Valeur Studio LLC ("we", "us") designs and builds websites, custom packaging and launch systems for small businesses, and is based in Florida. This policy covers our website, the private links we send to clients (for proposals, agreements, payments, reviews and approvals) and our emails. It does not cover websites we build for clients or other sites we link to. Contact: kazan@valeurstudio.com.

Information we collect, and why

From you.

  • Contact and business details you give us through our website forms, emails or calls, and a description of what you need. Form submissions are sent to us by email and kept in our business records.
  • What we need to prepare and deliver your project: scope notes, files and links you share, and your requests and feedback.
  • Records of what you approve or sign: your typed name and email, the date and time, the statements you agreed to and, if you draw one, your signature image. We also record your IP address and browser information with each approval or signature, so we can show who agreed to what and when.
  • Payment information. Payments are processed by third-party payment providers. We never see or store your full card number. We keep the amount, the status, the provider's reference numbers and, for recurring billing, the card brand, last four digits and expiry date. If you pay another way, such as a bank transfer, we keep the details you tell us about it.
  • Referral details, if someone refers you to us or you refer someone.

Our communications. We keep copies of the emails and drafts we send about your project. We do not use tracking pixels or open-tracking in our emails. If we invite you to book a call, the booking page is run by a third-party scheduling service, which receives what you enter. If we share a folder for project files, anyone who has the folder link can view and add files, so treat the link like a password.

Activity on the links we send you. When you open a private link we send you, such as a proposal, a payment page or a design preview, we record that it was opened, when, and which project it relates to, and our team may be notified. This is linked to you because the link was made for you. It is not anonymous, and it does not prove who opened it, because anyone with the link can. We do not use cookies for this, and we do not store your IP address or device details for this activity.

Technical information. Our website and hosting providers keep standard server logs, which can include IP addresses, request times and browser details. We use them for security and troubleshooting and to filter out automated traffic.

Free website audit

If you use our Free Website Audit, you give us your website address, business details and goals, and your name, email address and, optionally, phone number. To prepare the audit we review your public website and send your website address to a third-party website performance testing service. Your name, email address and phone number are not sent to it. The result is shown to you, sent to us with what you entered, and kept in our records. It is also kept in your browser for the visit so the results page can show it.

Reaching out to businesses

We contact some businesses we have not worked with before about our services. We use publicly available business information, such as business listings, business websites and public professional profiles. This can include names and contact details of owners or staff that a business has made public. We write and send these messages individually. If you do not want to hear from us, email kazan@valeurstudio.com and we will stop contacting you and note your request so that we do not contact you again. You can also ask what we hold about you or ask us to delete it (see "Your choices and rights").

Automated steps

Some routine account steps are automated by rule. For example, we send payment reminders, proposals stop being available after their stated expiry date, and if a scheduled payment stays unpaid after notice, hosted services may be paused until it is paid, as described in your agreement. If you think a step was taken in error, write to kazan@valeurstudio.com and we will review it.

How we use information

  • To respond to enquiries, prepare proposals, agreements and projects, and deliver and support our services.
  • To produce the audit you ask for.
  • To take payments, send receipts and manage billing.
  • To communicate with you about your project and follow up on enquiries and proposals.
  • To keep a record of what was agreed and to protect against fraud and misuse.
  • To find and contact businesses that may want our services.
  • To keep business records and meet legal, tax and accounting duties.

Who we share it with

We share information with third-party technology and service providers who help us run our business, such as for hosting, data storage, email, payments, scheduling, website testing, notifications and professional advice, and with others where the law requires. They process information on our behalf or under their own terms. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising based on your activity across other websites. We may disclose information if the law requires it, to protect our rights, or in a sale or reorganization of the business. You can ask us for the names of the providers we use.

Cookies and similar technologies

  • Our website and client pages do not set cookies, and we do not run analytics or advertising trackers.
  • Our website loads fonts and an image from third-party servers, which receive your IP address and browser details when you visit.
  • Some pages save a draft of what you are typing, or your audit result, in your browser so you do not lose it. This stays on your device.
  • Our payment provider may set its own cookies when you pay on its pages. Our team uses sign-in cookies in our internal systems; clients do not sign in.

Where it is stored, and how long we keep it

Our providers store information in the United States. We keep information for as long as we need it for the purposes in this policy and to meet legal, tax, accounting and dispute requirements. We do not currently delete information automatically or on a fixed schedule. We delete or anonymize information when you ask us to, as described in "Your choices and rights", and when we decide we no longer need it.

  • Agreements, approvals, signature records, payments and invoices: as long as needed for legal, tax, accounting and dispute purposes.
  • Link activity: as long as it is useful for the project and our records.
  • Enquiries, audit requests and business information we collected for outreach: as long as it may be useful for following up.
  • Notification history: until our team clears it.

Security

We use encryption in transit, limit access to our team, and keep client pages separate from our internal systems. Treat private links like passwords. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to tell you what we hold about you, correct it, delete it or limit how we use it, by writing to kazan@valeurstudio.com. We handle these requests by hand and will answer within a reasonable time. Where the law gives you the right to have your information deleted, we will delete it, except for records we must keep, such as agreements and payments. We may need to confirm who you are first.

Do Not Track. Browsers can send a "Do Not Track" signal. We do not collect information about your activity on other websites, and we do not respond differently to that signal.

If you are in the EEA or the United Kingdom

This section applies to personal information about people in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom.

Who is responsible. Valeur Studio LLC is the controller. Contact: kazan@valeurstudio.com.

Our legal bases. We use your information to perform a contract with you or take steps you ask for before one; for our legitimate interests in running and protecting a small business, following up on enquiries and proposals, keeping records of what was agreed, and contacting businesses that may want our services (you can object); to meet legal obligations; and with your consent where we ask for it, which you can withdraw at any time.

Sending information to the United States. We are based in the United States and our providers store information there, so information about people in the EEA or the UK is transferred outside those regions. Where EU or UK law requires a safeguard for this, we rely on the mechanisms our providers make available, such as standard contractual clauses or a provider's participation in the EU-US and UK data privacy frameworks. Ask us for details for a particular provider.

Your rights. You can ask to access, correct or erase your information, to restrict or object to our use of it (including where we rely on legitimate interests), to receive it in a portable form, and to withdraw consent. Write to kazan@valeurstudio.com. We will respond within one month; if we need longer, the law allows us to say so and explain why. We handle requests by hand.

Complaints. You can complain to the data protection authority where you live or work, or to the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

Children

Our services are for businesses and are not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell clients we are working with. Past versions are available on request.
